# Cost Analysis

Generated by [benchmarks/cost_calculator.py](../benchmarks/cost_calculator.py) — rerun it after changing any assumption. Two scenarios: **Scenario A** (conservative — AI pipeline sized as if every dial connects) and **Scenario B** (realistic — AMD filters ~70% of dials before they reach the AI pipeline, leaving a 30% answer rate typical of collections campaigns).

## The single most important finding in this cost model

**Human escalation dominates total cost — by roughly an order of magnitude over both AI compute and telephony combined.** At a 12% escalation rate and ~4 minutes of human handling time per escalated call, human labor cost swamps every other line item, even at a fully-loaded blended agent cost as low as $8/hr.

This inverts the intuition many teams start with ("the AI/GPU part must be the expensive part"). At this call volume, **`compliance`/`llm-gateway`'s ability to resolve calls without escalating (containment rate) is the single biggest cost lever available** — bigger than GPU choice, bigger than model quantization, bigger than telephony contract terms.

```
escalation_rate: 12% -> 8%   cuts human cost by ~33%   (~$21M/day -> ~$14M/day in Scenario B)
escalation_rate: 12% -> 5%   cuts human cost by ~58%   (~$21M/day -> ~$9M/day in Scenario B)
```

This directly motivates two priorities called out in [architecture.md](architecture.md) and [monitoring.md](monitoring.md): (1) the FSM+small-LLM design should be tuned and eval'd specifically to maximize safe containment without over-escalating on things it could actually handle, and (2) containment rate needs to be a first-class, continuously-monitored metric — a 4-point regression costs more per day than doubling the entire GPU fleet.

**Caveat on human cost realism:** $8/hr blended fully-loaded agent cost assumes largely offshore/distributed staffing; it will be materially higher in some markets/regulatory regimes and materially changes the total. This is the single most sensitive assumption in the whole model.

## Where the rest of the cost goes

### AI compute ($0.42M–$1.4M/day)
Dominated by `stt-service` GPU count (ASR runs continuously throughout the caller's speech, vs. `llm-gateway`/`tts-service` which only run briefly per turn). Biggest lever: streams-per-GPU (batching efficiency, quantization) — doubling ASR throughput/GPU cuts total AI compute cost by roughly a third. Self-hosting vs. commercial API pricing at this volume is a 20-50x difference, which is why self-hosting is non-negotiable at this scale (see [architecture.md](architecture.md)).

### Telephony ($1.8M/day)
`minutes/day × $/min`. Biggest lever: blended per-minute carrier rate, a procurement lever more than an engineering one — but it moves the total more than most engineering optimizations at this scale. Does not include number-pool acquisition/reputation-management costs (see [scaling.md](scaling.md)).

### Media servers ($5.3K/day)
Negligible — CPU-only commodity boxes. Not a cost lever worth optimizing.

## Cost-reduction priority order

1. **Maximize safe containment rate** (reduce escalation without sacrificing outcomes/compliance) — by far the largest lever.
2. **Improve AMD accuracy** — every percentage point improvement in filtering non-connects before the pipeline saves proportionally across compute *and* human cost.
3. **Negotiate telephony rates / optimize routing** at volume.
4. **Optimize GPU batching/quantization** — real savings, smallest of the four given human-cost dominance.

## What this means practically

At $21-67M/day depending on scenario, the literal 1B-calls/day target implies a cost structure that only makes sense at the value recovered per successful collection call, or as a system-design exercise rather than a real deployment plan (see [architecture.md](architecture.md)'s honest caveat). The transferable output is the **unit economics** ($0.02-0.07/call) — a real deployment would size call volume to the business's actual delinquent-account count (likely thousands to low millions/day) and use these same per-call unit economics to project a realistic budget.
